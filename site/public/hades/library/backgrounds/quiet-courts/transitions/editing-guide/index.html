Editing handoff · 24 fps

0–1314–1516–29
  1. Set the sequence to 24 fps. Import the 30 numbered PNGs as one image sequence (1920×1080, 1.25 s).
  2. Place the sequence above both shots. Cut between frames 14 and 15, both fully opaque.
  3. Interpret PNG as straight alpha. Check on light and dark footage; do not premultiply twice.
  4. For portrait or square use the matching SVG (SMIL capable renderer); native PNG bundles here are landscape only. Do not stretch.
