# HADES Architectural Section Cuts — PUBLIC REVIEW (2026-11-22)

Three NEW original 2D editable architectural background artworks, deliberately separate from Threshold, Quiet Spaces, Strata, Apertures, Folded, and Neutral Atlas. No imported logos or personal/private material.

- Cantilever & Void — 1920 × 1080 (16:9), dark mass / bone beam, fine vermilion.
- Sunken Gallery — 1080 × 1920 (9:16), bone facing / charcoal shaft, quiet plaque zone.
- Suspended Lintel — 1080 × 1080 (1:1), charcoal mass / bone lintel, fine vermilion.

**Only approved colour** is sRGB `#EF4B32`. Proposed neutrals: Dark `#131211`, Mammoth Bone `#E6D9C7`, Charcoal `#292723`, additional secondary plane values remain proposals. No typography inside these backgrounds; Mikhak was not tested.

These SVGs are editable and logo-free. For white original logos over bone, place the **unaltered** owner-supplied raster on a separate dark plaque; do not recolour, trace, invert, or reproduce the mark. No new monogram. Pattern02 stays private and Pattern03 stopped.
