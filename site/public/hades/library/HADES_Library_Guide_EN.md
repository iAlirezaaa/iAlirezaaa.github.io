Each generated asset includes editable **SVG**, raster **PNG** and a multi-layer **PSD**. The SVG describes a full design layout and is **NOT an official SVG logo**. The unchanged owner mark is embedded as a PNG layer, cropped only as a source-derived alpha where permitted. PSD layers are raster layers: edit text in the SVG.

Signature sRGB **#EF4B32** is approved; Classic/Modern are coordinated expressions of one realm identity. Supporting neutral values, font selection, copy, geometry and print specifications are pending owner approval. Mikhak is a working Persian font preference only; no font binaries are distributed.

HADES is the universe and HADES THE THIRD its creator/ruler within the owner's creative narrative, not a historical claim. Independent products including SkyVision preserve their own design systems; minor creator credits require permission.

Do not print or publish with invented client data. Replace bracketed placeholders with verified information.
