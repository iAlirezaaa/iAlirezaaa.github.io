# HADES — public brand overview
Status: Public reference | 2026-11-20

HADES is an independent creative universe: a realm and ecosystem for crafted stories, worlds and systems. HADES THE THIRD is the creator and current ruler within that universe.

The “third” is the owner's creative mythic sequence—Greek, Roman, then the present maker—not a historical assertion.

One identity has two coordinated expressions, Classic and Modern. The owner-approved signature accent is vermilion #EF4B32. Supporting neutrals, typography, layouts and slogans are not approved brand masters.

Independent works (including SkyVision) retain their own identities. A discreet “by HADES 3rd” signature may appear only when explicitly permitted; approximate visual prominence is secondary.

The original image files on the website are preserved without recoloring, redraw or vector tracing. They are public reference material, not certified SVG masters. A download is not permission to invent a replacement logo, claim affiliation, or reuse third-party work.
