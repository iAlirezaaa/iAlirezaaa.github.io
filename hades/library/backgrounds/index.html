Proposed area ratios

Dark applications: 78–85% obsidian/basalt, 12–19% skeletal/mammoth, 2–4% vermilion. Light applications: 75–84% mammoth/skeletal, 13–23% charcoal, 2–4% vermilion. These are design-starting points, not owner-approved rules.

Original logo on light contexts

For the original white mark on a warm-light background, place the unchanged original on a separate dark plate. Do not darken, invert, trace or recolor the official logo source. Validate actual micro legibility before using in production.
