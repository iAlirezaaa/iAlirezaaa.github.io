# HADES — Public Brandbook / REVIEW (2026-11-21)

## 01. The realm
HADES is a Universe Brand: a creative realm and ecosystem of original stories, films, tools and independent worlds. **HADES THE THIRD** is its maker and ruler. The Greek–Roman–third sequence is a personal creative mythology of the owner, not a historical assertion.

## 02. Identity structure
Classic and Modern are two expressions of the same identity, not separate brands. Independent products, including SkyVision, retain their own marks, palettes and interface design; optional `by HADES 3rd` credits need owner permission and remain visually discreet (approximately 10% relative prominence as a loose guideline).

## 03. Source logos
The four existing owner-supplied image originals and their manifest are the authoritative sources. No replacement monogram, automatic tracing, AI redraw, changed symbol or official vector-logo SVG may be generated from these review templates. A background-graphics SVG is **not** a logo SVG.

## 04. Logo scale
Prefer a deliberately small source-original mark and generous clear space. If the actual mark disappears at final size, omit it and rely on legible ordinary text rather than enlarging it excessively or redrawing its form. Do not print the low-resolution mark without a qualified source and print proof.

## 05. Colors
**Owner-approved fixed signature color:** sRGB `#EF4B32` (HADES Vermilion).
All precise neutral swatches, old gold, finishing and paper tones remain **PROPOSALS**. Confirm paper, profile, illumination and process colors before print production. Do not infer certified Pantone/CMYK matches from RGB values.

## 06. Typography
Working Persian font preference: *Mikhak* (میخک); exact family/weight/role and embedding/license evidence need separate owner approval and rendering tests. Latin Modern proposal: Inter. Classic editorial serif remains a proposed pairing. The downloadable SVG text is live/editable; PSD text is rendered as independently selectable raster layers, not a live Photoshop text object.

## 07. Background patterns
Architectural Arc 01 is available as a public **REVIEW** package with editable SVG and transparent version. Linear Engraved 02 remains privately reviewed, not published here. Patterns are support graphics with tiny accent touches and no repeated logo.

## 08. Stationery
Business card front/back and A4 letterhead dark/light remain in their public asset pages. Additional working REVIEW objects: DL envelope front/flap, folder cover/interior, A5 notebook, proposal cover and blank invoice layout. Every sample has editable SVG, PNG preview and layered PSD.

## 09. Social identity
Instagram square/portrait, stories/reels, carousel, X/LinkedIn headers and an intentionally quiet avatar. Real content and permissions must replace bracketed placeholders before publication. Avoid fabricated reach, clients, followers or awards.

## 10. Website & presentation
Four editorial layouts: website hero, section divider, editorial card and presentation cover. Maintain legibility at 320/390/430 CSS px, including Persian RTL and keyboard accessibility.

## 11. Visual exclusions
No gaming graphics, cyberpunk, neon/glow, stock mythology ornament, horns, skulls, flames, invented H/HH/III marks, large aggressive logos, AI cliché decoration or cluttered background overlays.

## 12. File workflow
**Original source → REVIEW/CONCEPT → separate owner approval per design → final PNG + print/device QA → verified original vector source → logo SVG (only if supplied and authorized).** Review website publication does not itself approve a print master or any font/palette rule.

## 13. Available file categories
[Categorized library](https://ialirezaaa.github.io/hades/library/) includes direct source SVG, PNG, PSD downloads and ZIP category packs. All new layouts are labeled REVIEW. Existing public card, letterhead and Pattern01 downloads are preserved.

## 14. Public vs private
Do not publish private contracts, internal decision logs, unpublished narrative assets, credentials or client files from the private branding repository. Templates with `[PLACEHOLDERS]` are drafts only.
